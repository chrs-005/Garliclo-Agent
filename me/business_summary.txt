GARLICLO | NADER GARLIC — BUSINESS SUMMARY
Garliclo (غارليكلو) — Air-peeled garlic | ثوم مقشّر عالهوا. No chopping. No peeling. No mess.

Garliclo, also known as Nader Garlic, is a Lebanese restaurant supply business led by Joseph Nader, part of a family name in garlic since 1980. It does one thing and does it well: peeled garlic. Garlic is in almost every Lebanese dish, from toum and shawarma to marinades and grills, yet peeling it by hand is one of the slowest and messiest jobs in any kitchen. Garliclo takes that job off the table. We supply restaurants, industrial kitchens, and butcheries (and other food businesses) with clean, ready-to-cook garlic cloves. Our promise: "You don't peel, we peel for you" (إنت ما تقشّر، نحنا منقشّر عنك).

Our signature is air peeling (مقشّر عالهوا). Instead of knives, soaking, or chemicals, our machines use bursts of compressed air to lift the skin off each clove. The cloves stay whole, firm, and unbruised, there is no added water, and the garlic keeps its natural sharp flavor. We also offer trimmed cloves (مقمّع), meaning the hard root end has been cut off, so the garlic is 100% usable with zero prep. Chefs trust Garliclo because it keeps their recipes consistent plate after plate, with garlic quality all year long. Garliclo is always ready, which makes it a must-have when opening any food and beverage business.

Our mission is to save Lebanese kitchens hours of tedious work every week while keeping quality, hygiene, and taste consistent, at honest and transparent prices. We give customers a real choice: garlic we peel ourselves with our air-peeling line, or imported peeled garlic from China at a lower price point, including a frozen option for long storage.
